What this can and cannot do

Cloudflare Workers carry TCP, not UDP. Most competitive shooters send gameplay over UDP and will not pass through any Workers-based tunnel — no panel can change that. What a pinned profile does give you: a route that never changes, no DNS lookup at connect time, no mid-match switching, and no extra relay hop. That is a real and measurable win for TCP games, launchers, and matchmaking.
